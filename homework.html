Being two levels behind means you basically lose B54 · play first

Something the maths turned up: because fights are short (about 3 hits), a level gap decides almost everything. Level-for-level you win about 85% of fights. One level down: 8%. Two levels down: 1%. Type matters just as much — a level-30 fire Growler genuinely loses to a level-16 Sharpfin!
